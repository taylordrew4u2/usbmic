# SobStage v1.12.1 — release candidate

SobStage records up to eight external microphones at once, each to its own
file plus a summed mix, and sends one low-latency monitor mix to everyone in
the room. This is a fix release on top of v1.12.0: two faults that could spoil
a take without telling you, both found by new automated tests that drive the
whole app.

> **This is a release candidate, not a general release.** Physical-hardware
> validation is still open. See **Known limitations** below and
> [`RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/blob/v1.12.1/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md) before you trust it with
> a recording you can't repeat.

| `SHA256SUMS` | Checksums for every file above. |

This release is not code-signed with a Developer ID or Authenticode
certificate, the same as every release before it. On macOS, after dragging the
app to Applications, run this once in Terminal before the first launch,
otherwise Gatekeeper says the app "is damaged":

```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/SobStage.app"
```

On Windows, SmartScreen shows "unknown publisher" on first run. Choose
**More info → Run anyway**. Verify the download against `SHA256SUMS` first.

To check a download, see [Download in the README](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/blob/v1.12.1/README.md#download).

## Fixes

- **A microphone that stops mid-take now says so.** If a microphone's audio
  stopped while it was still plugged in, SobStage wrote a line to its log and
  nothing else: the microphone's meter stayed lit, no warning appeared, and
  the silence in its track was put down to the computer being too slow. It is
  now treated exactly like a microphone that was unplugged. Its meter goes
  dark, the mid-take card tells you straight away, and the take's record says
  which microphone stopped and when.
- **A glitching microphone driver can no longer put a click in your
  recording or your headphones.** A driver that hands over a corrupt sample
  used to produce a full-volume click in that microphone's file, a click in
  the mix, and a click in everyone's headphones, and could wipe out the
  loudness figure for the whole take. Those samples are now written as
  silence and the headphone level is unaffected.

## Also in this release

- New automated checks that drive the real app through every screen,
  simulate a crash mid-take and a microphone dying mid-take, and push both
  mixes to their limits. They run on every change from now on.

Everything in [v1.12.0](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/releases/tag/v1.12.0) is included.
Full details: [`CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/blob/v1.12.1/CHANGELOG.md).

## Known limitations

- **No completed take from physical microphones has passed the release
  hardware matrix yet**, on any platform. The engine is covered by 587 unit
  tests and by simulated CoreAudio and WASAPI device layers, but simulation is
  not hardware certification.
- **Pulling the card mid-take** is handled in code, and an automated test
  makes the recording folder stop answering inside the real app at Record and
  at Stop. It has not yet been tested by pulling a real card on macOS or
  Windows.
- **HDMI capture cards:** a visible preview and a completed camera recording
  have not yet been verified on this release.
- **Phones and wireless receivers that present as ordinary USB audio** can't be
  told apart from an interface, so they may appear as inputs.
- **Monitor latency** has not been measured by loopback on real hardware.
- **Linux** is for early use and testing only.

## Updating and rolling back

There is no automatic update. Download the new version and replace the old
one. Your settings, microphone names and recordings are kept.

If this version causes you a problem, v1.12.0 and v1.11.0 remain available. Back up
`settings.json` before you downgrade. Rolling back never deletes recordings.

## Support and privacy

- Report a problem: [open an issue](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/issues/new).
  **Settings → Export diagnostics** creates a zip of logs, recent session
  details and device names. It never contains audio or video. Read it before
  posting it publicly.
- SobStage sends nothing anywhere. There is no crash upload, analytics or
  automatic update. See [`PRIVACY.md`](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/blob/v1.12.1/PRIVACY.md).
