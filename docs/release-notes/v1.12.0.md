# SobStage v1.12.0 — release candidate

SobStage records up to eight external microphones at once, each to its own
file plus a summed mix, and sends one low-latency monitor mix to everyone in
the room. This release is about failures you can see: if something goes wrong
during a take, SobStage says what happened, keeps the record of the take, and
does not freeze.

> **This is a release candidate, not a general release.** Physical-hardware
> validation is still open. See **Known limitations** below and
> [`RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/blob/v1.12.0/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md) before you trust it with
> a recording you can't repeat.

## Highlights

- **A pulled or stuck recording card no longer freezes the window.** Starting a
  take, stopping one, listing what was saved and exporting diagnostics now give
  up on a card that stops answering after five seconds and tell you which card
  it was. Launch, destination checks, write-speed checks and interrupted-take
  recovery already ran in the background.
- **Only external hardware is offered for recording.** Built-in Mac
  microphones, iPhone/Continuity, Bluetooth, AirPlay, network, virtual and
  aggregate inputs are left out. SobStage refuses an input it can't identify
  rather than guessing.
- **Cameras start switched off** and stay off until you turn them on. Your
  choice is remembered between launches.
- **An activity log goes with every take.** Starts, stops, failures and
  recoveries are written to `activity.log` in the take folder and in the backup
  copy, and shown under Advanced.
- **Devices are named when they come and go**, including swaps where the total
  number of devices doesn't change.

## Changes

- Mid-take alerts are ranked by severity: lost audio first, then warnings,
  recoveries and news.
- The first recording destination works straight away (`~/RECORDINGS`).
  Removable cards appear once they have been checked.
- Linux asks each ALSA device how many channels it really has. If it can only
  open mono, SobStage records mono and says so, instead of writing silent
  extra tracks.
- The buffer-size ladder now actually counts overruns. Before this, it never
  moved.
- macOS, Windows and Linux ask the operating system whether SobStage may use
  the microphone and camera, and report what it answered instead of assuming
  it said yes.
- Combining video with the mix after a take runs in the background. Quitting
  never waits on a stuck ffmpeg, and an unfinished combined file is removed,
  not left looking complete.

## Fixes

- A take whose folder couldn't be read after Stop is no longer called empty.
- Camera previews: one native preview per device; same-name capture cards open
  the right device; a black or lost HDMI signal is reported instead of shown as
  live.
- REC is shown only after the camera confirms it is writing. Stop waits for the
  movie to finish before the take's details or the combined file claim it.
- Fixed A/V sync drift in combined files, a false camera alert, and loudness
  advice that disappeared when you pressed Stop.
- Interrupted-take recovery counts only files that will actually play.
- Several messages that never reached the user now do: which camera failed to
  start, a movie that didn't finish, and why the card stopped.

Full details: [`CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/blob/v1.12.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Known limitations

- **No completed take from physical microphones has passed the release
  hardware matrix yet**, on any platform. The engine is covered by 576 unit
  tests and by simulated CoreAudio and WASAPI device layers, but simulation is
  not hardware certification.
- **Pulling the card mid-take** is handled in code, and an automated test
  makes the recording folder stop answering inside the real app at Record and
  at Stop. It has not yet been tested by pulling a real card on macOS or
  Windows.
- **HDMI capture cards:** a visible preview and a completed camera recording
  have not yet been verified on this release.
- **Phones and wireless receivers that present as ordinary USB audio** can't be
  told apart from an interface, so they may appear as inputs.
- **Monitor latency** has not been measured by loopback on real hardware.
- **Linux** is for early use and testing only.

## Updating and rolling back

There is no automatic update. Download the new version and replace the old
one. Your settings, microphone names and recordings are kept.

If this version causes you a problem, v1.11.0 remains available. Back up
`settings.json` before you downgrade. Rolling back never deletes recordings.

## Support and privacy

- Report a problem: [open an issue](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/issues/new).
  **Settings → Export diagnostics** creates a zip of logs, recent session
  details and device names. It never contains audio or video. Read it before
  posting it publicly.
- SobStage sends nothing anywhere. There is no crash upload, analytics or
  automatic update. See [`PRIVACY.md`](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/blob/v1.12.0/PRIVACY.md).
