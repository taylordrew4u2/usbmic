# SobStage v1.13.6 — release candidate

SobStage records up to eight external microphones at once, each to its own
file plus a summed mix, and sends one low-latency monitor mix to everyone in
the room. This is a Mac fix release on v1.13.5: seven more fixes
that matter during a show.

> **This is a release candidate, not a general release.** Physical-hardware
> validation is still open. See **Known limitations** below and
> [`RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/blob/v1.13.6/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md) before you trust it with
> a recording you can't repeat.

| `SHA256SUMS` | Checksums for every file above. |

This release is not code-signed with a Developer ID or Authenticode
certificate, the same as every release before it. On macOS, after dragging the
app to Applications, run this once in Terminal before the first launch,
otherwise Gatekeeper says the app "is damaged":

```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/SobStage.app"
```

On Windows, SmartScreen shows "unknown publisher" on first run. Choose
**More info → Run anyway**. Verify the download against `SHA256SUMS` first.

To check a download, see [Download in the README](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/blob/v1.13.6/README.md#download).

## What's new

- **The live mix now goes to your headphones, not the MacBook speakers.**
  If you have never picked an output in Settings, SobStage now picks
  headphones in the Mac's headphone jack, or whatever output macOS is playing
  through, instead of the built-in speakers. AirPods or a Bluetooth speaker
  that connects on its own, or the headphone jack of a mic you switched off,
  no longer takes over the monitor, though you can still choose any of them by
  hand. The output SobStage picks by itself also stays put when macOS moves
  its default output, for example when a USB mic is plugged in mid-show.
- **Camera previews no longer cover the recording cards.** The live
  camera picture used to sit on top of the save prompt, the Saved card, the
  red recording alert and the RECORDING banner, hiding their text and buttons.
  The previews now go blank while any of these is on screen and come back when
  it closes; the cameras keep running and recording throughout.
- **A mic unplugged and plugged back in during a take is no longer
  reported as recording while its track is silent.** The app now tells you it
  can't rejoin the take in progress and that pressing Stop, then Record brings
  it back. A mic plugged in for the first time mid-take is no longer described
  as being in the headphones.
- **A mic that stops sending audio for a few seconds mid-take and then
  starts again is recorded again.** Before, if a mic's audio paused for 5
  seconds or more (for example when the MacBook slept and woke), its track
  stayed silent for the rest of the take and the app wrongly warned that the
  computer was dropping sound. Now its track goes live again as soon as its
  audio comes back, and the activity log says so.
- **A camera that took the app down while starting is no longer restarted
  by opening the Cameras panel.** SobStage now leaves that camera off on every
  launch, not just the next one, until you switch it off and back on in the
  Cameras panel. Opening the panel, starting a take or switching on a
  different camera no longer starts it.
- **Record comes back on its own after you fix a mic that wouldn't
  open.** If no microphone could be opened (the interface was at the wrong
  rate, or another app was holding it), SobStage now tries again by itself
  after you fix the problem. Before, you had to replug the interface or
  restart the app.
- **A camera that can't start recording now says so straight away.** If a
  camera lost its connection just as you pressed Record (for example a
  Continuity iPhone being picked up), the app now reports "Couldn't start
  recording video from" that camera. Before, its tile showed STARTING for the
  whole take, no video was written, and Record stayed blocked for 15 seconds
  after Stop.

**Before a show:** set your microphones and headphones to the same sample rate
as SobStage in **Audio MIDI Setup** (48 kHz is safest), allow the microphone
prompt, and make a short test take.

Everything in [v1.13.5](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/releases/tag/v1.13.5) is included.
Full list: [`CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/blob/v1.13.6/CHANGELOG.md).

## Known limitations

- **No completed take from physical microphones has passed the release
  hardware matrix yet**, on any platform. The engine is covered by 683 unit
  tests and by simulated CoreAudio and WASAPI device layers, but simulation is
  not hardware certification.
- **Pulling the card mid-take** is handled in code, and an automated test
  makes the recording folder stop answering inside the real app at Record and
  at Stop. It has not yet been tested by pulling a real card on macOS or
  Windows.
- **HDMI capture cards:** a visible preview and a completed camera recording
  have not yet been verified on this release, including with every camera now recording by default.
- **Phones and wireless receivers that present as ordinary USB audio** can't be
  told apart from an interface, so they may appear as inputs.
- **Monitor latency** has not been measured by loopback on real hardware.
- **Linux** is for early use and testing only.

## Updating and rolling back

There is no automatic update. Download the new version and replace the old
one. Your settings, microphone names and recordings are kept. 

If this version causes you a problem, v1.13.5 remains available. Back up
`settings.json` before you downgrade. Rolling back never deletes recordings.

## Support and privacy

- Report a problem: [open an issue](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/issues/new).
  **Settings → Export diagnostics** creates a zip of logs, recent session
  details and device names. It never contains audio or video. Read it before
  posting it publicly.
- SobStage sends nothing anywhere. There is no crash upload, analytics or
  automatic update. See [`PRIVACY.md`](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/usbmic/blob/v1.13.6/PRIVACY.md).
